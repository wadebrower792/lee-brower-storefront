# GTA VI Made $3.79 Billion in One Day — the Biggest Entertainment Launch Ever

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, November 21, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI did not just break video game records. It broke entertainment records, period.

Rockstar Games said Friday that the game took in $3.79 billion in retail sales in its first 24 hours, based on the company's own estimates through the end of Thursday, November 19 — launch day. That makes it the biggest entertainment launch in history, across any medium. The game also sold 32.5 million copies before it even came out, a pre-release record across all platforms.

To put that in plain terms: in one day, a video game made more money than the highest-grossing movie of all time made in its entire run in theaters. Avengers: Endgame took in about $2.94 billion at the worldwide box office over its whole run, according to media analytics firm Rentrak. GTA VI passed that in 24 hours.

## How big is big?

The last game in the series, GTA V, made about $800 million on its first day back in 2013, from around 11 million copies. GTA VI made nearly five times that. The standard edition sells for around $80 in the U.S.

This was the first new Grand Theft Auto in 13 years, and the wait showed. Fans spent more than 132 million hours watching the game on Twitch, YouTube, and Kick on launch day alone, according to analytics platform Stream Charts. The game also scored a near-perfect 99 on Metacritic, the review-aggregation site.

Rockstar's parent company, Take-Two Interactive, has good reason to celebrate. The game is estimated to have cost more than $1 billion to make, which would make it one of the most expensive entertainment projects ever produced. At $3.79 billion in a single day, it paid for itself before lunch.

## What it means for everybody else

Here is the part that matters beyond the gaming world. Entertainment is a business like any other, and this launch shows where the money is. A game that took more than a decade to build, with a development budget bigger than most Hollywood blockbusters, just out-earned Hollywood's biggest hit in a single day. Studios, streamers, and publishers are all watching the same number.

There is also a lesson in patience here. Rockstar delayed this game more than once — it was first pushed from May 2026 to November 2026 — and fans waited 13 years since the last one. The company did not rush a half-finished product out the door to hit a date, and the launch numbers say the wait paid off.

One honest note for parents: this is a crime game set in a fictional Miami, rated M for Mature. It is not a kids' game, no matter how many kids are talking about it at school. The sales numbers are real, and so is the rating.
