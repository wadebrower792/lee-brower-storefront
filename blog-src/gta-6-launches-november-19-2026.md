# GTA 6 Lands Thursday — Four Days Out, Here's What's Actually Confirmed

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, November 15, 2026

Four days from now, the biggest video game launch in history hits the shelves. Grand Theft Auto VI arrives Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. If you have waited thirteen years for a new numbered GTA game, the wait is just about over — and this time, the date is holding.

Rockstar Games and its parent company Take-Two Interactive have both confirmed the date in writing. Take-Two repeated it in its quarterly results release on November 9, and chief executive Strauss Zelnick told investors the launch was "ten days away." Rockstar's own game page says "Coming November 19, 2026." As of November 9, no official source had moved the date again.

That matters because this game has already been delayed twice. It was first expected in fall 2025, then pushed to May 26, 2026, and then pushed again to November 19. Two delays is why plenty of players kept asking whether a third one was coming. The answer, as of this week, is no.

Here is what is confirmed. The Standard Edition costs $79.99, and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99, according to Take-Two's pre-order announcement. Digital pre-loading opened November 10, and Rockstar's support page says the download needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on the device. If you bought a physical copy, know this: the box contains only a download code. There is no disc inside.

What is not confirmed is just as important. There is no PC version announced, no PC date, and no PC price or system requirements. A GamesBeat report in October said Rockstar plans a PC release in the first quarter of 2027, citing four unnamed industry sources, but Rockstar has not confirmed it. Treat that as a report, not a promise. Reviews are also unconfirmed: reports say the review embargo lifts November 18, the day before launch, but Rockstar has not announced that either.

The demand is real either way. Take-Two's Zelnick said pre-orders were running far ahead of the company's initial expectations. Sensor Tower estimated 7.71 million digital pre-orders by November 3 — that is an outside estimate, not a company count, but it gives you a sense of the scale. Other game publishers spent the whole fall getting out of the way: August, September, and October were packed with releases as studios cleared the decks before GTA's debut, and several games delayed themselves into early 2027 rather than launch next to it.

The road here was not clean. Rockstar's co-head of development Aaron Garbut said seeing early work leak felt "weirdly invasive" and was emotionally hard to deal with, and more leaks followed in October ahead of Rockstar's extended look at the game. Leaks and hype are part of every big launch now, but this one has been bigger than most because the game has been thirteen years coming.

So what should you actually do before Thursday? If you pre-ordered digitally, start the download now — 212 GB is no joke on a slow connection. If you are buying physical, remember it is a code in a box, so you will still need the download. And if you are a PC player, do not plan your week around November 19, because your version has no date yet.

Thirteen years, two delays, and one of the most anticipated launches ever. Thursday, it is finally real.
