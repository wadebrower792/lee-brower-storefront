# Social Security Checks Go Out Today — If Your Birthday Falls on the 11th Through the 20th

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, November 18, 2026

If your birthday falls between the 11th and the 20th of the month, your November Social Security payment lands today, Wednesday, November 18. That's how the Social Security Administration runs it: payments go out on the second, third, or fourth Wednesday of the month, depending on the day you were born.

Here's the full November schedule, straight from the SSA's published payment calendar. People born on the 1st through the 10th were paid on Tuesday, November 10 — a day early, because the usual second Wednesday, November 11, was Veterans Day, a federal holiday. Today's group, birthdays on the 11th through the 20th, gets paid on the third Wednesday, November 18. The last group, birthdays on the 21st through the 31st, gets paid on the fourth Wednesday, November 25.

That schedule only covers people who started collecting benefits in May 1997 or later. Folks who started before May 1997 are paid on the 3rd of the month, and they got their November payment on November 3.

Now the numbers people actually ask about. The most any retiree can collect in 2026 is $5,181 a month — but that takes waiting until age 70 to claim, and earning at or above Social Security's taxable maximum, $184,500 in 2026, for close to 35 years. Very few people hit that ceiling. Someone claiming at full retirement age in 2026 tops out at $4,152 a month, and someone claiming at 62 tops out around $2,969. Full retirement age is 67 for anyone born in 1960 or later.

The average is a lot lower than the maximum. The SSA's 2026 cost-of-living adjustment fact sheet puts the estimated average monthly benefit for all retired workers at $2,071 as of January 2026, after the 2.8% COLA for this year.

One more thing to know: a bigger raise is coming. The SSA announced a 3.6% COLA for 2027 on October 14, 2026. It takes effect with December 2026 benefits, which are paid in January 2027 — so it won't show up in this month's check.

If your payment hasn't shown up by the end of the day, don't panic right away. Direct deposits can land at different times depending on your bank. You can check your own payment date in your my Social Security account or on the SSA's 2026 payment calendar.
