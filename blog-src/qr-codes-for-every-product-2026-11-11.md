# Scan a Code, Shop the Store: Every Product in My Store Now Has Its Own QR Code

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, November 11, 2026

Here's a simple idea. You see something of mine on a flyer, on a truck, on a jobsite table, or on somebody's phone — you point your camera at it, and you're in the store. No typing a long web address. No searching around for the right page. One scan, and you're looking at that exact product, with the price right there.

That's what I just finished. Every product in my store now has its own QR code — all 84 of them — plus two codes for the whole store itself. There's a page on the storefront that holds every one of them in one place: the [QR Codes hub](https://wadebrower792.github.io/lee-brower-storefront/qr-codes.html).

It works like this. Open your phone's camera and point it at the code. A link pops up at the top of your screen. Tap it and you land on that product's page, where you can read what it is and buy it if you want it. You don't need a special app. The camera that came with your phone already knows how to do it.

The two store codes are the ones that matter most. One opens my Gumroad store, where checkout happens. The other opens the full storefront here, where you can browse the whole catalog first — the Bid Kit, the Playbook, ContractorOS, the prompt packs, the books, all of it. Those two are at the top of the hub page, big and easy to scan.

Why do it this way? Because most folks don't shop from a desk. They shop from their phone, standing in a parking lot or sitting on a tailgate. A QR code meets them where they are. If a code is on a flyer at a supply house or on the back of my truck, the store is one tap away instead of a web address nobody is going to type in by hand.

Every code was checked before it went up. All 86 of them were scanned and confirmed to open the right page — the right product, the right price. If a code didn't work, it didn't make the page. That's the same rule I hold everything in the store to: it works before it goes live, or it doesn't go live.

So if you've got a product you like, you'll find its code on the hub page. And if you're the kind of person who hands out flyers or puts a sign in a window, the two store codes are yours to use. Scan one, and you're in.
