# 10 Million Setups: What Meta's Muse Numbers Actually Say

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, October 30, 2026

Meta's AI assistant Muse passed 10 million setups in the United States and Canada in about six weeks. That figure comes from earnings reporting on October 30, 2026, and the wording matters: the report says people "set it up." That is not the same as 10 million daily users, 10 million downloads, or 10 million paying customers, and this post does not upgrade it into any of those things.

## What the earnings reporting actually said

The same reporting said more than 25 percent of weekly Muse users shop through it. Again, that is a behavior figure from earnings reporting, not a claim that every shopper buys something. Meta's third-quarter 2026 capital spending was reported at $34.91 billion, with a 2026 guide of $135 billion to $145 billion. On the earnings call, Mark Zuckerberg said Muse is "very compute-intensive to run" today and that Meta expects to improve its cost efficiency "by more than 10X in the next year," which would enable profitability "once the revenue lines are at scale." The first two parts of that sentence are a stated cost and a forecast. The last part is conditional on revenue that does not exist yet at that level. The reporting itself flagged that nothing reviewed establishes Muse will become a successful commerce-fee or subscription business, and that part is not settled.

## The hotel booking claim, checked

Agoda announced on October 28, 2026 that its AI travel assistant is now connected to Muse for destination discovery, hotel bookings, and itinerary management, for Muse users in Singapore and Japan on mobile and the web. Agoda described itself as the first travel platform in the region with a live connection. What is often repeated wrong is the property count: Agoda's overall inventory spans more than 6 million accommodation properties, but the company does not say all of those are available through the Muse connector. The connector is announced for Singapore and Japan only, and Meta says partner experiences roll out progressively. Singapore is Muse's first announced Asia-Pacific market, with early connectors including Shopee, Grab, Singapore Airlines, Agoda, ShopBack, Klook, Trip.com, and Lazada. Support for Meta's AI glasses in Singapore is planned but not yet available, with no launch date given.

## What this means for a working person

The honest read is narrower than the headlines. An assistant that 10 million people set up in six weeks is a real distribution event, and a quarter of weekly users shopping through it is a real behavior signal. Neither one proves a business model, a price, or a result for any particular user. If you use an AI assistant for work, the useful question is not how many people set it up. It is what it does for your next job: does it draft, follow up, and keep records straight, and does it stop and ask before it spends, sends, or commits anything in your name? That is the standard this blog holds every AI claim to, including Meta's.

Sources: Tech Insider earnings reporting, October 30, 2026; Agoda announcement via Travel PR News, October 29, 2026; TechNode reporting on Meta's Singapore announcement, October 30, 2026.
