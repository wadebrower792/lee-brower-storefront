# Apple's Black Friday Deal Is a Gift Card Up to $300 — Here's What You Actually Get

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, November 21, 2026

Apple announced its 2026 Black Friday event on November 19, and the deal is the same shape it always is: no price cuts, but a gift card with your purchase. The event runs four days, Friday, November 27 through Monday, November 30, 2026, online, in the Apple Store app, and in stores.

The gift cards range from $25 to $300, depending on what you buy. The biggest card — $300 — comes with an eligible 16-inch MacBook Pro with an M5 Pro or M5 Max chip. That top amount is up from $250 last year, a $50 increase.

## The full breakdown

Here is what Apple is offering in the U.S., by product:

- **Mac:** 16-inch MacBook Pro (M5 Pro or M5 Max) gets $300. The 14-inch MacBook Pro gets $250. The 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Air with the M5 chip get $200. The Mac mini gets $100.
- **iPhone:** iPhone Air gets $100. iPhone 16 and iPhone 17 get $75. iPhone 17e gets $50.
- **iPad:** iPad Pro with the M5 chip gets $125. iPad Air with the M4 chip gets $100.
- **Apple Watch:** Apple Watch Ultra 4 gets $100. Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch SE 3 get $50.
- **AirPods:** AirPods Max 2 get $75. AirPods Pro 3 get $50. AirPods 5 get $25.
- **Home and accessories:** The full-size HomePod gets $50, the HomePod mini 2 gets $25. Select Beats products get $25 to $75. For the first time, Apple is also offering a $25 gift card with select third-party accessories from brands like Anker, Belkin, Mophie, Nimble, Rains, and Twelve South.

Apple limits the offer to two gift cards per eligible product category per customer.

## Read the fine print before you buy

The most important thing to understand is that the gift card is for a future purchase. It does not lower the price of the thing you are buying today. You pay full price now, and you get a card to spend later on apps, music, iCloud storage, accessories, or another device.

A few products are excluded entirely, including some of Apple's newest: the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone Duo, MacBook Neo, the latest iPad mini, Apple TV 4K, iMac, and Mac Studio. Refurbished products are ineligible, and the offer cannot be combined with education pricing or veterans and military discounts.

And here is the practical truth, the one the deal sites will tell you straight: better discounts on Apple products are often available from resellers like Amazon during Black Friday week. Apple's gift card is only a good deal if you were already planning to buy the product from Apple at full price and you will actually use the card later. If the gift card is the reason you are buying something you did not need, it is not a deal — it is a $25 to $300 reason to spend hundreds or thousands of dollars.

Black Friday falls on November 27 this year. If an Apple device is already on your list, the gift card is real money back in your pocket for later. If it is not on your list, no gift card makes it a bargain.
