# Anthropic Put a Price on Its IPO: $70 to $78 a Share, Worth Over $1 Trillion

By Wade Lee Brower Jr, November 10, 2026

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI assistant, filed new paperwork for its planned stock market debut, and this time it put real numbers on the table. The company said it plans to sell more than 800 million shares, with existing shareholders selling another 25 million, at an expected price of $70 to $78 apiece. That puts the implied value of the company above $1 trillion — which would make it one of the biggest publicly traded companies in America the day it arrives.

That is a big number, but it is smaller than the number people were talking about earlier this year. Some investors had expected a valuation as much as twice that. Just last week, reporting said investors were pushing back on a $1.5 trillion figure, down from $2 trillion talked about in October. The filing lands the company above $1 trillion, not at $2 trillion. The market, in other words, is still deciding what an AI company is worth — and the answer keeps coming in lower than the first guess.

The filing is an amended S-1, the document a company files with regulators before it sells shares to the public. It does not set a final date for the offering. What it does is tell the world: here is how many shares, here is the price range, here is the size of the deal. The next step is the roadshow, where the company pitches big investors and the price gets set for real.

## Why it matters to regular folks

You do not have to own a single share of Anthropic for this to matter. Claude is one of the AI tools a lot of working people now use every day — writing bids, answering emails, sorting paperwork, building estimates. When a company like this goes public, it gets a pile of cash to build with, and it also gets a pile of new bosses: shareholders who expect growth every quarter. That changes how a tool behaves over time. Features get priced. Free tiers get squeezed. The tool that helped you for free starts asking for a subscription.

There is a second lesson here for anybody building something of their own. Anthropic is pushing ahead with this offering while AI stocks have been swinging hard — the Nasdaq fell more than 5 percent in a single day last week, and chip stocks took the worst of it. The company is not waiting for calm weather. It is going while the window is open, because windows close. That is the same rule on a jobsite: when the weather holds and the material is on the truck, you pour the concrete. You do not wait for a perfect day.

The timing also says something about the AI business itself. Anthropic is one of the companies at the center of the data center buildout — the same buildout that has Google spending $15 billion in Malaysia and contractors booked out across the country. AI is not a fad anymore. It is concrete, steel, power lines, and cooling systems, and now it is also a trillion-dollar stock listing. The money is real, the buildings are real, and the tools on your phone are part of it.

## What to watch next

The price range is not the final price. If investors line up, the offering could price at the top of the range or above it. If they hesitate, it could come in lower. Watch the roadshow and the final pricing — that is where the market gives its honest verdict. And watch what happens to Claude itself in the months after the listing. A public company answers to its shareholders first, and the product follows.
