ContractorOS on your phone — the whole job in your pocket

ContractorOS is a Lee Brower digital product, built by a working construction superintendent. It is not a Siteworx product, and Siteworx Southern Division, LLC is a separate construction company. The phone app is the field half of the same system: the cloud/desktop copy is the system of record, and the phone writes through it.

What the phone app does

  • Talk your daily log in by voice — the record that gets you paid, filed to the right project.
  • Job photos filed to the right project from the jobsite.
  • Estimates and invoices in one place, with the job's documents and drawings at hand.
  • Crew scheduling the whole company can see.
  • Built-in trade calculators — no separate app, no per-seat add-on.
  • Takeoffs, punch lists, and approvals without going back to the desk.
  • AI office on the phone — an AI secretary and PM that answers from the job record.

Honest notes

  • Bid leads originate on desktop; the phone works the job, it is not where bid leads start.
  • Offline queue and AR measurement were built in phone app builds and are awaiting the builder's own on-device verdict — they are not claimed as shipped features on this page.
  • No Procore or Autodesk sync, no CPM scheduling, no marketplace integration yet.
  • Plan scan enhances and assembles plan pages into documents — it does not measure from them.
