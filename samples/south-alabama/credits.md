## Stand-ins and substitutions — stated plainly

- **Opp, Luverne, Greenville** use truthful stand-ins (the snake species, the food,
  the flower), captioned as what they are. No free photo of the Opp Rattlesnake
  Rodeo, Luverne's World's Largest Peanut Boil, or a Greenville camellia planting
  could be found on Wikimedia Commons.
- **Evergreen** shows the town's real 1940 post office. No legally free photo of
  the Conecuh Sausage plant or gift shop exists on Commons; the post office is an
  honest, in-town landmark and is captioned as exactly that.
- **Monroeville**: the first Highsmith frame pulled (LCCN2011646788) turned out to
  be an infrared/black-and-white exposure; it was replaced with Highsmith's color
  frame of the same view (LCCN2011646797).
- The **hero** is the real Blue Lake in the Conecuh National Forest — the lake in
  the Andalusia entry — not a generic sunset.
