Fieldwire is a field-first tool, and its free Basic tier is a real way to try plan viewing and task tracking at no cost. Where the bills diverge is headcount: Fieldwire charges per user, so a growing crew's bill grows with it, while ContractorOS tiers are flat. Note also that older Fieldwire price figures still circulate in some listings; the figures above are the ones current listings agree on as of this check.

Where ContractorOS wins

  • One app from bid to punch. Fieldwire is strongest at plans, tasks, and punch in the field; ContractorOS adds bids, takeoffs, estimates, invoices, and an AI office in the same app.
  • Flat pricing. No per-user multiplication as the crew grows.
  • 1-month free trial on every tier.

Where ContractorOS honestly lacks

If any of these is a must-have, do not pick ContractorOS today.

  • No Procore or Autodesk sync.
  • No Fieldwire-grade plan viewer with sheet compare at the free tier — Fieldwire's free Basic tier already covers plan viewing.
  • No critical-path (CPM) scheduling.
  • No marketplace integration yet.
  • Plan scan enhances and assembles plan pages into documents — it does not measure from them.
  • Photo stamps: designed, not built.
  • Offline queue and AR measurement: built in phone app builds and awaiting the builder's own on-device verdict — not claimed as shipped features on this page.
  • Company customization (your own company name and logo on top, "Powered by ContractorOS" beneath) is not proven yet and is not claimed here.
  • Small-shop scale: it is not an enterprise platform, and it does not pretend to be one.

Who should pick which

Pick ContractorOS if you want one flat-priced app for bids through punch plus an AI office, and you do not need Fieldwire's plan-viewing depth or its integrations.
Pick Fieldwire if your core need is field plan viewing, task tracking, and punch across a larger crew, especially if you want to start on a free tier.
