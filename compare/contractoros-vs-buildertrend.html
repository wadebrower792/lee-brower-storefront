Where ContractorOS wins

  • Published pricing, no quote wall. You can see $49–$799 and start a trial without a sales call.
  • Small-shop all-in-one. Bids, takeoffs, daily logs with photos, documents and plan scans, drawings, punch lists, approvals, time clock, and an AI office — in one app, on phone and desktop.
  • Phone-first field use. Built to be run from the phone you already carry on the job, not only from a desk.
  • One flat price, no per-seat charges. ContractorOS tiers are priced by crew size, not per user.

Where ContractorOS honestly lacks

If any of these is a must-have, do not pick ContractorOS today.

  • No Procore or Autodesk sync.
  • No Buildertrend-style homeowner selections and warranty workflow.
  • No critical-path (CPM) scheduling.
  • No marketplace integration yet.
  • Plan scan enhances and assembles plan pages into documents — it does not measure from them.
  • Photo stamps: designed, not built.
  • Offline queue and AR measurement: built in phone app builds and awaiting the builder's own on-device verdict — not claimed as shipped features on this page.
  • Company customization (your own company name and logo on top, "Powered by ContractorOS" beneath) is not proven yet and is not claimed here.
  • Small-shop scale: it is not an enterprise platform, and it does not pretend to be one.

Who should pick which

Pick ContractorOS if you run a small shop, want one app for bids through punch, want published pricing, and do not need homeowner selections, warranty tracking, or enterprise integrations.
Pick Buildertrend if you are a residential builder or remodeler whose margin depends on homeowner selections, allowances, and warranty work, and you are comfortable with quote-only pricing.
