IRONPORT

An original open-city game. Drive the streets, run the jobs, dodge the law. This city is ours.

How to play: Left stick to move / drive. ENTER CAR to jack a ride. Cause trouble and the cops come — lose them to cool off. Finish missions for cash, spend it on faster cars in the GARAGE.

Controls: stick = move & steer (up = gas, down = brake/reverse) · ENTER = get in/out · PUNCH = swing on foot · RUN = sprint · Keyboard: WASD/arrows, E enter, Space brake, F punch, Shift run.
